Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Kati is an offline child immunization record keeper. This policy is short because the product is simple: we do not collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Kati has no accounts, no sync server, and no analytics SDK.

  • Child profiles, dose records, and preferences stay in on-device storage (SQLite) on your phone.
  • We do not upload immunization records to any server operated by Kati.
  • Optional exports (PDF record, backup file) only leave the device when you explicitly share them through your phone’s share sheet.

Purchases

In-app subscriptions are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. Purchase status is checked on-device through their SDKs so the app can unlock Kati Plus features. Kati does not receive your payment card details.

Notifications

Local reminders are scheduled on your device only. They are not sent through a push notification server operated by Kati.

Children’s data

Records you enter about a child remain under your control on your device. Delete the app (or clear its data) to remove them from that phone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email listed on the App Store product page for Kati.
